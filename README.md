A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The Dark Lord of the Hollow Throne has kept his seat for three hundred years, and it shows in his knees, his feet, and his diary: the hero's village burns Thursday, no, Friday. He never says he is weary. He just keeps asking the hero to sit down.
